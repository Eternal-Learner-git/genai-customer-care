Account Security Policy

If a customer suspects unauthorized access to their account, support should immediately guide them to reset their password and enable two-factor authentication if not already active.

Accounts showing suspicious activity (such as logins from unrecognized locations) may be temporarily locked as a precaution. The account owner will be notified by email and asked to verify their identity before the account is restored.

Support agents should never ask customers for their password directly. Identity verification should be done through the customer's registered email or phone number only.

Customers who wish to permanently delete their account and associated data can do so by submitting a verified request; this action is irreversible and all order history will be lost.
