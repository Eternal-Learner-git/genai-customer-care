Delivery Policy

Standard delivery takes 3-5 business days within the same country. Express delivery is available at checkout for an additional fee and typically arrives within 1-2 business days.

If a package is marked as delivered but the customer has not received it, they should first check with neighbors and the building reception, then contact support if the item still cannot be located within 24 hours.

Delivery delays caused by weather, customs, or courier issues are outside the company's direct control, but support can escalate the shipment for tracking updates and, where appropriate, offer a partial refund of shipping costs for significant delays.

Customers can update their delivery address only before the order has shipped. Once an order has shipped, the address cannot be changed.
