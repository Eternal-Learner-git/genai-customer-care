Billing and Charges Policy

Customers are billed at the time of order confirmation, not at the time of shipment. Duplicate charges are rare but can occur due to network retries during checkout; these are automatically reversed within 3-5 business days, or manually by support if flagged.

Subscription services renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the renewal date. Cancelling a subscription stops future charges but does not refund the current billing period unless required by local consumer protection law.

Customers can view and download their full billing history from their account settings at any time. For disputes about a specific charge, support should ask for the transaction ID and date to investigate.
